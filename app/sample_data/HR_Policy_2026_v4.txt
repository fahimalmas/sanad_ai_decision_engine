# GLOBAL IT & HR OPERATIONAL POLICY (INTERNAL USE ONLY)
Document ID: HR-POL-2026-V4
Effective Date: January 15, 2026 | Version: 4.2
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL

## Section 1.0 - Scope and Objective
This document outlines standard operating procedures for all full-time, part-time, and contracted personnel across global operating entities. All guidelines governing the handling, storage, and protection of company assets and human capital adhere to international data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA).

--- PAGE 2 ---
## Section 2.0 - Working Hours, Remote Work & Attendance
2.1 Standard Office Hours: Normal working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday.
2.2 Remote Work Eligibility: Employees who have passed the 90-day probationary period are eligible to apply for up to 3 remote work days per week, subject to direct manager approval.
2.3 Cross-Border Remote Work: Working from a foreign jurisdiction is strictly prohibited without prior written authorization from the Chief People Officer (CPO) and Tax Compliance Director due to permanent establishment risks.

## Section 4.0 - Expense Reimbursements & Capital Expenditures
4.1 General Principles: All business-related expenses incurred on behalf of the company must be documented with itemized receipts and submitted within 30 days of occurrence.

--- PAGE 18 ---
## Section 4.2 - Remote Worker Hardware & Technology Stipend
All remote worker hardware claims up to $2,500 are permissible under the annual tech stipend. Claims exceeding $1,000 require secondary director approval from a Level 4 Director prior to submission to finance.

Failure to attach the countersigned Form B-12 will result in automatic rejection by the AP automated system. 

Original itemized receipts must accompany every submission. The reimbursement will be processed within the subsequent monthly payroll cycle following final approval.

--- PAGE 19 ---
## Section 4.3 - Travel & Entertainment
Business class travel is only authorized for intercontinental flights exceeding 8 continuous flight hours. All other flights must be booked under standard economy fares. Daily meal per-diem is capped at $75 per day.

## Section 7.0 - Maternity, Paternity, and Parental Leave
7.1 Maternity Leave: Female employees with at least one year of continuous service are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. An additional 4 weeks of unpaid leave may be requested.
7.2 Paternity Leave: Male employees are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave to be taken within the first 6 months following childbirth.
