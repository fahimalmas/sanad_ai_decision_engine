# GLOBAL PROCUREMENT & VENDOR GOVERNANCE POLICY
Document ID: PROC-POL-2026-FINAL
Effective Date: February 1, 2026 | Version: 2.4
Classification: INTERNAL / LEGAL RESTRICTED

## Section 1.0 - Purpose and Applicability
This policy governs all procurement contracts, master service agreements (MSAs), statements of work (SOWs), and vendor onboarding procedures across all subsidiaries and operating business units.

--- PAGE 12 ---
## Section 3.0 - Commercial Terms and Financial Governance
3.1 Vendor Selection: Any purchase order exceeding $50,000 must undergo a competitive bidding process involving at least three qualified vendors.

--- PAGE 13 ---
## Section 3.2 - Standard Payment Terms & Early Termination
Payment Terms must strictly be Net-60 days from invoice date. Early payment discounts (e.g., 2% Net-10) are encouraged. Early termination penalty is capped at 5% of the remaining unbilled contract value. 

Under no circumstances should any operating division agree to payment terms shorter than Net-60 without explicit written derogation from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

--- PAGE 14 ---
## Section 3.3 - Liability and Indemnification
Maximum liability shall not exceed the total contract value paid over the preceding 12 months. Any uncapped liability clauses from external vendors are strictly prohibited.
All vendors must carry commercial general liability insurance with a minimum limit of $2,000,000 per occurrence.

--- PAGE 25 ---
## Section 5.0 - Data Security and Cloud Hosting
Vendors processing corporate or customer personal data must maintain SOC2 Type II certification or ISO 27001 compliance. Data must remain encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
